Pickle Labs is a three-person AI consultancy run out of a converted garage in Beirut, Lebanon. Founded in 2026, we build LLM features and AI agents for small and mid-size companies, and we audit the ones that stopped working. We are remote-first and have already shipped more than 15 projects for clients in Lebanon, the Gulf and Europe.

Our mission is to make AI systems that do what they claim to do. Most failed AI projects do not fail because the model is weak; they fail because nobody measured what the system actually does. Our unique value proposition is simple: we tell you the truth about your AI before you ship it. Every engagement ends with evidence, meaning traces, test results and a plain-language verdict.

We offer four services: Agent Autopsy, a one-week audit of an AI system that is not working; Build Sprint, a four-week build of one agent or LLM feature with an evaluation suite; Retainer, monthly engineering hours for fixes and improvements; and Prompt Clinic, a two-hour live session to repair prompts and tool definitions. Detailed pricing, deliverables and policies are in the full business profile.

The team is Dr. Karim Haddad (founder and lead engineer), Layla Nasser (infrastructure and deployment) and Sami Karam (evaluations and observability). Every project starts with a free 30-minute scoping call. To get started, leave your name, email and a line about what you need.
